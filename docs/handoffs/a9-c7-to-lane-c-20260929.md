## Where I went wrong, so it is not repeated

1. **My R2 finding was a measurement artefact.** I reported "one click sends the sections list from `scrollTop = 400`
   back to 0". Playwright's `locator.click()` scrolls the target into view first, and the rows I clicked were cut off
   at the top. With a fully visible row the list does not move. R2's code (`preventScroll`, the reveal confined to
   the picker's own scroll root) is harmless and defensible, **not** a proven fix.
2. **I mislabelled two mid-panel rows as "the last visible row"** in the evidence file. That is why the bottom of the
   list — where both defects live — was never rendered until QA reproduced it. Rows are now labelled by section name
   and trigger position.
3. **My own `max-h` instruction caused the clipped list.** Replacing the body's definite `h-100` with a maximum left
   the flex container's height indefinite, so the ScrollArea viewport never became smaller than its 79 options: the
   list was clipped, not scrollable, and ~76 rooms were unreachable. A maximum is not a height.
4. **The round asked to correct two false comments shipped a third.** QA caught that the deletion I ordered never
   happened and that the new helper's comment stated the opposite sign from its code. All three are corrected; the
   suite is 9/9.
